Before you start

DepthScan needs a TrueDepth iPhone and a few ordinary magnets. If you sideloaded the beta, trust the developer profile first.

Hardware

iPhone 12 Pro or later · 2–3 small magnets · flat desk · part roughly 2–15 cm

Install / trust

Developer Mode on + restart, then Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Trust the DepthScan developer. Full install steps: Try the beta.

Permissions

Allow Camera and Motion when prompted — TrueDepth + magnetometer won’t start without them.

01 / PLACE

Lay the phone face-up

Open DepthScan and put the phone flat on the table, screen facing up. Keep it still — CoreMotion waits until the device is level and steady.

  • Screen faces the ceiling
  • No wobble — the app advances automatically when stable
  • Don’t hold the phone in your hand for scanning

02 / MAGNETS

Stick 2–3 magnets on the part

When the phone is steady, DepthScan asks you to attach magnets. Spread them out — not in a straight line — so rotation can be solved uniquely.

  • Any small neodymium magnets / stickers work
  • Tap Magnets attached when ready
  • Or skip and track rotation visually only (less robust)

Tip: different sizes help, but aren’t required.

03 / BASELINE

Capture the Earth baseline

Move the part away for a moment so the magnetometer reads ambient field with no magnets nearby. The app does this automatically after you confirm magnets are attached.

  • Hold the part clear of the phone
  • Wait for the spinner to finish
  • Then bring the part back for pose capture

04 / POSES

Hold 6 reference poses

Rotate the part to clearly different angles. At each pose, hold steady and tap Capture pose. TrueDepth supplies orientation ground truth while the magnetometer records the field.

  • 6 poses total (~20 seconds)
  • Change angle a lot between captures
  • App then solves magnet positions & strengths

05 / SCAN

Hold the part above the screen

After calibration, the live mesh builds on screen. Hold the part in the TrueDepth beam and rotate slowly. Hands can stay in frame — Vision masks them out.

  • Watch coverage fill on the bar
  • Optional: Add another phone / Join a session for multi-phone coverage
  • Keep the desk phone still the whole time

06 / REVIEW

Review coverage

When coverage looks solid, DepthScan offers to finish or keep scanning. Magnets leave tiny shadows — you can relocate them for a second pass, or finish now.

  • Keep scanning — fill more angles
  • Move magnets & fill gaps — second pass
  • Finish — start export

07 / FILL GAPS

Move magnets, scan again

Optional but recommended. Move the magnets to new spots (still spread out), tap I moved them, hold still while DepthScan confirms, then rotate again under FILLING GAPS.

  • Exact new positions don’t matter
  • You can skip and finish with small gaps
  • Tap Finish when the second pass looks good

08 / EXPORT

Build the final mesh

DepthScan runs Object Capture on the saved frames and writes the mesh. When done you’ll see Scan complete — start a new scan anytime.

  • Progress shows while the mesh builds
  • Output lands as a saved mesh file on device
  • Pro / Studio unlock more export formats — see Pricing
