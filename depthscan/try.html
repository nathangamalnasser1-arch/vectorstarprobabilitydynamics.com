Install in 8 steps

  1. Download the app file

    Click Download DepthScan.ipa at the top of this page and save it somewhere easy to find, like your Desktop.

  2. Windows only: install iTunes and iCloud

    Sideloadly needs Apple's drivers to talk to your iPhone. Install iTunes and iCloud using the download links on sideloadly.io (the versions from Apple's website, not the Microsoft Store).

    On a Mac, skip this step.

  3. Install Sideloadly

    Download Sideloadly for Windows or Mac from sideloadly.io and install it. It's free.

  4. Connect your iPhone

    Plug your iPhone into the computer with the cable and unlock it. If it asks “Trust This Computer?”, tap Trust and enter your passcode.

  5. Install DepthScan with Sideloadly
    • Open Sideloadly. Your iPhone should appear in the iDevice list.
    • Drag DepthScan.ipa onto the IPA icon in the top-left.
    • Type your Apple ID email in the Apple account box.
    • Click Start, then enter your Apple ID password and the 6-digit code Apple sends you if asked.

    Wait until the log at the bottom says Done. The app icon now appears on your iPhone, but it won't open yet.

    Your password goes only to Apple, to create a free signing certificate. If you'd rather not use your main Apple ID, a second free Apple ID works too.

  6. Turn on Developer Mode

    On your iPhone open Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, switch it on and tap Restart. After the restart, tap Turn On and enter your passcode.

    The Developer Mode option only shows up after step 5. You only do this once.

  7. Trust your Apple ID

    Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID email under Developer App, then tap Trust and confirm.

  8. Open DepthScan

    Tap the DepthScan icon. Allow Camera, Motion and Local Network when asked — the app needs them to scan. Lay the phone face-up on a table to begin.

Good to know

The app stopped opening after a week.
Apps installed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days. Plug in your phone and repeat step 5 — your scans stay on the phone. Sideloadly can also refresh it automatically while your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi.
Sideloadly doesn't see my iPhone.
Unlock the phone, accept “Trust This Computer”, and try another cable or USB port. On Windows, make sure iTunes is the version from Apple's website (step 2), then restart Sideloadly.
It says “Untrusted Developer” or the app closes right away.
Steps 6 and 7 aren't done yet — turn on Developer Mode and trust your Apple ID, then open the app again.
“Maximum number of apps” error.
A free Apple ID can have 3 sideloaded apps on a phone at once. Delete one you don't use, then try again.
